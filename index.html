PRACTICE, WITHOUT THE PRESSURE

One answer.
Three new perspectives.

Prepare questions from your role and experience. Record your answer, then choose how you want to reflect.

  1. 01

    Watch your presence.

    Replay with the audio off. Notice your expression, posture, and camera connection.

  2. 02

    Listen to your delivery.

    Hide the video. Hear your pace, pauses, and how your story comes together.

  3. 03

    Bring it together.

    Review the full answer. Continue past a highlight, skip a review, or try another question.

Evidence-based coaching cues

Speech recognition may send audio to your browser’s speech provider. TypeSafe’s data policies apply to the transcript and estimates you send.

01 You choose what to share.+

Coaching is off until you opt in. You can record and review without analysis. The homepage demo never accesses your camera, microphone, resume, or account.

02 Feedback is a practice cue.+

Camera and voice estimates can be noisy. They do not measure emotion, disability, personality, confidence, or hiring suitability. Missing or uncertain evidence is not assigned a score.

03 Your review, at your pace.+

Use video-only or audio-only playback, continue through individual highlights, or skip any review. This site supports keyboard navigation and reduced motion; accessibility work is ongoing.

04 Clear about what exists today.+

AdaptIQ is an early product. We do not claim security certifications or institutional deployments. Saved history, live intervention, personalized coaching profiles, and program dashboards are previews, not current studio features.
